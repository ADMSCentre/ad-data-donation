Thank you for participating in our study The Australian Ad Observatory.

### Participation

Participation in this activity is voluntary and optional. If you do not wish to take part, you do not have to, and this will not impact your participation in the wider study that you have already completed.

If you choose to participate in this activity, you will receive an additional $25 to recognise this additional voluntary contribution of time and knowledge to this project.

### Purpose of Data Donation

Your advertising data, in combination with the ads you collect on the Australian Mobile Ad Toolkit App, help us understand how digital advertising operates.
